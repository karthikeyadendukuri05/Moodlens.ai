MoodLens AI 2.0 ✨

Discover the emotion behind your words using a simple multilingual sentiment analysis model.

English • Hindi • Telugu • Hinglish • Teluglish

Educational prototype using a small Naive Bayes model and sentiment phrase matching. Predictions can be inaccurate, especially with slang, sarcasm and unfamiliar spellings. No text is sent to a server.
